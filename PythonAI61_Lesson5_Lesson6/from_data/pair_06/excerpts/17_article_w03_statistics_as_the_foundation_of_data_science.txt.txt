Illustration of data (image by author) Nield (2022) describes data such as a photo that provides a snapshot of a story. Sustained reality and context are not fully captured, nor are the infinite number of variables driving the story [2]. So, it can be interpreted that data is information obtained from all events that can be used as information. Data can be in the form of numbers or categories (such as good, bad, high, low, and so on). There are several characteristics of good data, such as objective, representative, up-to-date, relevant, and reliable.
